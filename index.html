About Me

Welcome to my website! I'm a Physics Ph.D. student at The Ohio State University who is graduating in May of 2025. Throughout my time at OSU, I have worked on computational particle-in-cell simulations and machine learning methods to optimize laser-induced ion acceleration in Dr. Christopher Orban's high energy density physics group. As someone who has studied physics, mathematics, and computer science, I have a diverse skillset in quantitative/statistical methods, scientific programming, and physical intuition that has been honed over the course of my degree.

Please check out the navigation menu to see some data dashboards I've created and my other website for more information about the work I did while at OSU.
