Modified Fuchs et. al. Model

Throughout my Ph.D. I worked on constructing a synthetic dataset based on the Fuchs et. al. empirical model for proton acceleration. This model predicts proton energies in the so-called target normal sheath acceleration regime in which an ultra-intense laser pulse interacts with a micron-scale thickness target to produce highly energetic protons. I published a paper on this work in November of 2024 and a further analysis is under peer-review. The most recent work adds physics to account for pre-expansion of a laser-heated target. This is caused by a pre-pulse of a certain contrast that arrives before the main laser pulse. The dashboard below shows the trends of the modified Fuchs et. al. model in predicting either the maximum accelerated proton energy or average accelerated proton energy. These two quantities are predicted as a function of four inputs: target thickness, main-pulse peak intensity, contrast between pre and main-pulse, and distance from the target to the laser's peak focus.
